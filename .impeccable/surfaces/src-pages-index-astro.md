# Homepage

Scope: `src/pages/index.astro`, the portfolio homepage. Mode: **Persuade**. A hiring evaluator should leave ready to contact Virginia on LinkedIn (https://www.linkedin.com/in/virginialovotti/).

Audience and job: recruiters and employers scanning paid social proof. Content: the seven Dr. Woof ads in `src/ads.ts`, each presented as its own case with **equal weight**. Source order is kept. The Conversion or Hook tag stays visible, and The Sign gets no headline treatment. Display rules from PRODUCT.md hold: account-relative percentages in parentheses, days on air only, no spend or revenue.

Build path: code-led (project default).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every ad is a claim with its own proof beside it. The page refuses the category's moodboard wall of creatives and its single hero case.

OWN-WORLD: The neutral scale runs from #FAFAFA to #111. The only color comes from the creatives themselves, each framed as a 9:16 screen on a soft radial ground. Type is Satoshi in two weights with sentence-case headings. Metric panels are hairline-ruled with tabular numerals. Controls are ghost buttons; the single primary button is solid near-black with a chartreuse (#A6C23F) dot. The accent appears four times: the two primary buttons, the account-average marker in the reading key, and a case card reached from the index.

STORY: The visitor learns what Virginia makes, reads seven matched cases (segment, angle, awareness, result versus the account average), believes the numbers because each sits beside its creative, and contacts her.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the left, a two-line headline sits above a one-line deck, a "Contact on LinkedIn" primary button, and a "See the cases" ghost button. On the right, a case index panel lists all seven cases in equal rows, each with a thumbnail, the ad name, its type, its headline metric, and days on air. A reading-key strip runs below.

FORM: Monochrome Product Marketing, catalog world `id: digital-design-canon-monochrome-product-marketing`, user-pinned. seed: none (user-pinned catalog world; no roll ran, and Worlds stores the catalog id instead of a seed key). Signature interaction: each index row links to its case card, which lifts into a highlighted state when reached. Each card rises softly as it enters the viewport. Video cases play inline from a ghost play control.

FINISH: Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished. This build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
